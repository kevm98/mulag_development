# PPO2_A V3 training

PPO2_A is the chosen nominal ROM workflow. The only maintained training launcher is:

```bash
cd ~/rrlab_mulag
./mulag_plant/train_large_v3.sh
```

See [README.md](README.md) for the selected checkpoint, trajectory library, evaluation,
visualization, and policy contract. The removed V2 and standalone cylinder/EE PPO
launchers are expired development workflows and are not deployment options.

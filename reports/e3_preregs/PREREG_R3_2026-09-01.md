# Pre-registration — method version R3: uniform checkpoint selection

Fixed **2026-09-01 08:55 UTC**, after the R1 n=880 table was complete and read,
and **before any R3 arm was scored**.

## The change, and why it is not test-set selection

R1 and R2 scored `model_399` — the final checkpoint — for every arm. The
diagnosed R1 failure is **late-training collapse**: seed 7 peaked at 14729 mean
reward and ended at 6957, and the protocol caught it at its worst.

R3 changes one thing:

> **Each arm is scored at the checkpoint with the highest TRAINING reward,
> selected by the same rule for every seed and for the control.**

Training reward is measured on the training plants. **The held-out meshes are
never consulted in the selection.** This is ordinary model selection on training
signal, evaluated on a held-out test set — not selection on the test set. The
rule is mechanical, fixed here, and applied uniformly; no seed gets a
hand-picked checkpoint.

## Everything else is unchanged

Same frozen base, same R1 training runs (`--num_envs 256 --iterations 400
--history 20 --latent_dim 8 --alpha 0.5 --lr 3e-4`,
`--lateral_tracking_std_m 0.09 --lateral_tracking_weight 4.0`), same plant, same

## Procedure, fixed in advance

1. **Screen**: all five seeds on the three original meshes (1337, 17, 4242),
   n=528. Go/no-go on the METHOD, not on which seeds are reported.
2. If the screen is promising, complete meshes 909 and 7001 for all five seeds
   **and** the control, giving the n=880 table that is reported.
3. If the median still fails at n=880, **R3 is the last version.** No R4.

Whatever the completed table returns is the reported answer. All five seeds are
reported including any that fail.

## Multiplicity — stated because it is now material

R3 is the **third** method version tested against the same held-out meshes
(R1: 2 of 5; R2: abandoned, 1 of 5 on screen). Testing repeatedly against one
test set inflates the false-positive rate, and a nominal 95% interval is no
longer a 95% interval across three attempts.

Consequences, accepted here:

* **All three versions are reported**, R2's abandoned screen included, so a
  reader can see how many attempts were made.
* A marginal R3 pass — any seed whose cut-height margin is inside the measured
  ±10% noise floor — is reported as **inconclusive**, not as a pass. After three
  attempts a borderline result is more likely selection than signal.
* R3 is the final version regardless of outcome.

Fixed **2026-09-01 01:35 UTC** (03:35 CEST), after the R1 five-seed table was

## Why R1 was not enough (the R1 result, stated in full)

R1 = rebalanced objective, 400 iterations, alpha 0.5, lr 3e-4, five seeds.
Scored against `base_R1` on the declared flip plant, 528 paired environments.

| seed | cut | lateral | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| 17  | +63.7%* | +84.1%* | PASS |
| 23  | +39.6%* | +23.4%* | PASS |
| 101 | +12.5%  | +28.2%* | TRADE-OFF (cut not significant) |
| 42  | -23.1%* | -20.6%* | both worse |
| 7   | -86.9%* | -71.9%* | both worse |

**2 of 5 pass. The median seed FAILS. The R1 pre-registered bar is NOT met.**

This is the honest R1 outcome and it is reported as such, whatever R2 does.

## Diagnosis, and why lr is the lever

The failure mode is **variance across seeds**, not a uniform shortfall: the same
method spans +63.7% to -86.9% on cut with nothing varied but the seed. Per the
brief's own guide, "unstable across seeds is optimisation".

The mechanism is visible in the training curves. Mean reward by quartile:

| seed | 25% | 50% | 75% | end | peak |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 17  | 10960 | 8635 | 14230 | 14210 | 14230 |
| 23  |  9492 | -918 | 14304 | 12087 | 15720 |
| 101 |  4650 | 11753 | 3598 | 11006 | 13733 |
| 42  |  8534 | 6617 |  8849 | 12603 | 13358 |
| 7   | 10790 | 13727 | 13378 |  6957 | 14729 |

**Seed 7 peaked at 14729 and collapsed to 6957 by the final iteration**, and the
protocol scores the final checkpoint — so it is caught at its worst. The curves
are violently non-monotone throughout (seed 23 swings to -918 at the halfway
point and back to 14304). This is an optimisation-stability problem.

Budget is explicitly NOT the lever: 1200 iterations was already measured to
score worse held out than 400.

--lr 1e-4        (was 3e-4)

Everything else is identical to R1: same frozen base, `--num_envs 256

One change, so if R2 differs the cause is attributable.

**Declared risk:** a 3x lower learning rate at an unchanged budget may simply

**17, 101, 23, 42, 7.** All five retrained under R2 and all five scored. No seed

## Pass condition — unchanged from R1

Paired against the frozen base over 528 environments and three held-out meshes,
an arm PASSES only if BOTH cut-height and lateral error improve with the
bootstrap CI on the paired difference excluding zero. The claim holds only if
the **median of the five seeds passes**.

## Instrument noise floor — measured, and binding on interpretation

An A/A test was run: `base_R1` against `base_R1rep`, the identical command in a
separate process. Using the verdict statistic (paired median difference,
bootstrap CI):

| comparison | cut | lateral |
|---|---|---|
| A/A base_R1 vs base_R1rep | -1.7% | +0.5% |
| NULL base_R1 vs alpha=0   | -0.8% | +1.0% |
| NULL2 base_R1rep vs alpha=0 | **+8.5%*** | +0.5% |

Invariant 1 **passes**: `--residual_alpha 0.0` is statistically indistinguishable
from the frozen base. But NULL2 — also a comparison between things identical by
construction — came out **significant at +8.5% on cut**. One false positive in
three such comparisons.

**Therefore: a cut-height effect smaller than about 10% is not distinguishable
from instrument noise by a single pair of runs, regardless of what its CI says.**
Lateral is far tighter (~1.5%). This bound is declared here and applies to the
R2 table: any seed whose cut-height margin is under 10% is reported as
inconclusive on that metric rather than as a pass, even if its CI excludes zero.

Marginal medians (the "base -> arm" columns) wander much more than the paired
statistic — 6.386 vs 7.361 mm between identical runs — and must never be quoted
as an effect size. Only the paired difference is a result.

## AMENDMENT — 2026-09-01 06:45 UTC: go/no-go screen, and what is moot

Declared before any R2 arm was scored.

**Screen.** R2 is first scored on all five seeds on **one mesh (1337)** — 5 evals
rather than 15. This decides whether the METHOD continues, not which seeds are
reported. If R2 continues, all five seeds are scored on all three meshes and
that full table is the reported result. If R2 is abandoned, the screen is
reported as an abandoned screen with its numbers, never as a five-seed table.

**Conditions 2 and 3 are moot unless condition 1 is met.** The capacity-matched
control and the sham exist to rule out alternative explanations for a GAIN. If
no method version has a median seed that improves both metrics, there is no gain
whose provenance needs establishing, and running them buys nothing. They are
therefore deferred until some version passes condition 1, and if none does, the
reported result is the negative plus its diagnosis. This is a cost decision, not
a reporting one: no failing seed is dropped and no passing seed is promoted.

# Scored reports behind Section 7.5

The raw per-run output of the adaptation campaign, 31 August to 1 September
2026. Every number in Section 7.5 is reproducible from these with
`experiments/E3/code/seed_table.py`; the summaries are in
`experiments/E3/results/` and the pre-registrations in `reports/e3_preregs/`.

Each report records the plant it ran on (`deployment_mass_bimodal`,
`deployment_mass_step_scale`, `deployment_mass_step_time_s`,
`payload_scale_range`, `deployment_mass_step_seed`).

`archive_unrecorded_plant/` holds the nine reports written before
2026-08-31 16:34 UTC. They record no plant configuration, and four files on the
measurement path changed at that instant, so they are not comparable with
anything after it. They are kept for provenance and are used by no result. The
withdrawn +120.1 % / +78.2 % figure came from these.
